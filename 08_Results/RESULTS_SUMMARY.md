\# Zgamma Coplanarity Study - Results Summary





\## Background Study



Photon conversion background was investigated using:



\\\[

\\gamma \\rightarrow e^+ + e^-

\\]



The purpose was to test whether converted photons can satisfy the coplanarity selection criteria.





\---



\# Dataset



MadGraph generated events:



10000





Selected leading photons:



9560





Simulated photon conversions:



94





Generated charged particle tracks:



188





\---



\# Coplanarity Classification Result



Accepted conversion events:



64 / 94





Overall acceptance:



≈ 68%





This demonstrates that photon conversion backgrounds can pass the coplanarity requirement.





\---



\# pT Dependence



\## 5-20 GeV



Events:



43





Accepted:



27





Acceptance:



62.8 ± 7.4 %





\---



\## >20 GeV



Events:



51





Accepted:



37





Acceptance:



72.5 ± 6.2 %





\---



\# Main Observation



Photon conversion backgrounds are not completely rejected by the coplanarity selection.



Higher transverse momentum photons show slightly higher acceptance, consistent with reduced curvature effects.



However, larger statistics are required before making a strong pT-dependent rejection claim.
